This policy explains the app's local storage, Android backup, visits to our legal-information pages and communication with us.

2. A brief overview

You can use the checklist without creating an account. Your reflections are stored in the app's private storage on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your reflections, and the app does not provide an account-based synchronisation service.

The app contains no advertising or embedded third-party analytics service. We do not sell your reflection data or use it for advertising.

However, local storage does not necessarily mean that data always stays on the same device. Android's backup and device-transfer features may include app data, as explained in section 4. Opening external pages or contacting us also involves separate processing.

3. Data stored by the app

The app stores the information needed to provide your reflections:

  • The checklist points you select.
  • The day or date range assigned to an entry.
  • Any optional note you enter.
  • Whether an entry is a draft or completed.
  • Internal entry identifiers and creation and update timestamps.
  • Your appearance preference and information recording which walkthroughs you have already seen.

This storage enables you to reopen, edit and delete entries and retain your preferences. No name, email address or account registration is required to use these features.

We do not receive or review the content of your locally stored reflections. Optional notes are for your own use. Please avoid entering unnecessary information about other people.

Reflections may be personal or sensitive. Protect access to your device, particularly if it is shared with someone else.

4. Android backup and device transfer

The app currently permits Android's operating-system backup features. Depending on your device, system provider, account and settings, Android may include the app's database and preferences in a cloud backup or transfer them to another device.

On devices using Google's backup service, this may involve storage associated with your Google Account. Other manufacturers may offer their own transfer or backup services.

These features are provided by the operating system or relevant platform provider. We do not receive access to your backup through the app. Backup availability, timing, retention and restoration depend on the relevant service; we cannot guarantee that a backup exists or can be restored.

You can review the available backup settings on your device. Disabling future backups does not necessarily delete copies that already exist. Consult the relevant provider's settings and privacy information for management and deletion options.

5. Storage duration and deletion

Local reflection entries remain in the app until you delete them or the app's local data is removed. Deleting an entry removes it and its associated selections and note from the app's active local database.

You can also remove local app data using Android's app settings. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local data. Operating-system backups, transferred copies and copies you have created separately are subject to their own deletion processes. Reinstalling the app may restore data from an available system backup.

Deleting local data does not automatically instruct every backup provider to erase all existing copies.

We use this hosting to provide reliable access to information about the app. Our legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, based on our legitimate interest in secure and reliable website delivery. We do not add visitor analytics, advertising trackers, embedded media or external web fonts.

9. Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies to processing for which we are responsible, the following bases are relevant:

  • Article 6(1)(b): processing necessary to provide an agreed service or answer a request made before entering into an agreement.
  • Article 6(1)(f): our legitimate interests in answering general enquiries, maintaining app quality, securely providing our information pages and protecting against misuse. We consider the effects on your rights when relying on this basis.
  • Article 6(1)(c): processing necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations.

These bases do not by themselves authorise processing special categories of personal data. Additional conditions apply where such processing falls within our responsibility.

The app stores or accesses information on your device to provide the functions you request, such as saving an entry or retaining an appearance setting. In Germany, strictly necessary storage or access for a service expressly requested by the user is addressed by section 25(2), number 2 TDDDG.
